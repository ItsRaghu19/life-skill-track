# Learning Process

## Question 1: What is the Feynman Technique?

The Feynman Technique is a learning method in which you explain a concept in simple words, identify gaps in your understanding, and study those gaps until you can explain it clearly.

## Question 2: In the Barbara Oakley TED Talk, what was the most interesting story or idea for you?

The most interesting part was Barbara Oakley's story of struggling with maths and science in school, but later changing her learning approach and becoming an engineering professor. It shows that we can improve our ability to learn with the right methods and effort.

## Question 3: What are active and diffused modes of thinking?

- **Active (focused) mode:** A state of concentrated attention in which we work directly on a problem or study a topic carefully.
- **Diffused mode:** A relaxed state of thinking in which the mind makes broader connections and can approach a problem from a different perspective.

Both modes are important: focused mode helps us learn details, while diffused mode helps us connect ideas and gain understanding.
